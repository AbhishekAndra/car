FULL TRANSPARENCY: WHAT'S COVERED VS EXCLUDED

We believe in zero hidden clauses or surprise fine print when an accident occurs.

WHAT IS FULLY COVERED:
  • Accidental collisions, crashes, flips, and physical impacts with any object or vehicle.
  • Natural calamities including flash floods, heavy cyclones, storms, earthquakes, and landslides.
  • Total vehicle theft, robbery, housebreaking, and malicious vandalism damage.
  • Self-ignition, sudden electrical fire, lightning strike, and explosion.
  • Transit damage while vehicle is on a transport trailer, flatbed, or rail cargo.
WHAT IS LEGALLY EXCLUDED:
  • Driving under the influence of alcohol, drugs, or intoxicating substances.
  • Operating the vehicle without a valid legal driver's license.
  • Normal mechanical wear-and-tear, gradual brake pad thinning, or tire tread erosion.
  • Illegal street racing, sanctioned track competitions (unless Track Day endorsement added).
  • Direct nuclear irradiation, sovereign war, rebellion, or military invasion damages.
